# ADR-004: Free, openly licensed content only
**Decision.** Ingest only sources whose licence is machine-readable and permits streaming: Internet Archive netlabels (CC BY/BY-SA/BY-ND/CC0/PD),
LibriVox (public domain), podcast RSS (publisher syndication; stream/download only, never mixing) and synthetic Sound Lab audio.
NonCommercial and unknown licences are skipped. **Consequences.** No commercial catalogue; rights are explicit per asset; legal review of
source terms is still required before launch.
