# Product requirements (summary)

Source: `requirements/IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN (1).md`. Product scope adopted for this build: **free and openly licensed content only**
(Creative Commons/public-domain music, LibriVox public-domain speeches, publicly syndicated podcasts, synthetic Sound Lab audio).

* Five tabs: Home, Discover, AI Studio, Podcasts, Library; persistent mini-player; full player with queue, shuffle/repeat,
  speed (spoken), sleep timer, like, bookmarks, transcript/chapters, output route (AirPlay picker on iOS; system output switcher on Android), quality.
* Onboarding: welcome, sign in / create / guest, interests, notification opt-in, terms & content availability notice.
* Motivation hub: topics (discipline, confidence, career, resilience, leadership, fitness, mindfulness), daily featured speech,
  short/long sessions, speaker pages, bookmarks and shareable timestamp links (`vibefusion://listen/<id>?t=<s>`).
* AI Studio: prompt + examples, duration, mood, energy curve, genres, speech toggle/topic, AI or manual plan, timeline,
  gain/mute/solo/fades/offsets/loop/pan/role, ducking, preview, save, rights-gated export, recipe-only sharing.
* Library: liked music, saved speeches/episodes, followed shows, playlists, mixes, downloads, history.
* Profile: account, guest upgrade, taste, quality/data saver/downloads, explicit filter, analytics & AI consent, export, delete, licences.
